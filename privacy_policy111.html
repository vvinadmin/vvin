1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Giti 12 (the "App"), developed by Sandeep K Apps ("we", "us", "our"), handles information when you use our mobile application. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

📱 Information You Provide

The App does not require registration, login, or any account. Player names you enter are used only on your device, during gameplay, and are never transmitted to us or stored on any server.

📢 Information Collected by Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays advertisements through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Google and its partners may automatically collect certain information to serve ads, including:

  • Device identifiers such as the Advertising ID
  • Device information (model, operating system version, screen size)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address (country/city level)
  • Ad interaction data (ads viewed/clicked)

This information is collected and processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive or store this data on our own servers.

🎮 Game Data

All game progress (board state, settings, sound preferences) is stored locally on your device only. We have no access to it and it is never uploaded to any server.

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

7. Opting Out of Personalized Ads

You can limit ad personalization at any time:
