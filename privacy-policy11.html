V SHARE

Ultra Speed Local File Sharing — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 12, 2026

1. Overview

V SHARE ("we", "our", or "the app") is a local file-sharing application that lets you transfer files directly between your own devices — Android, iOS, and PC — over your local Wi-Fi network. This policy explains what information the app handles and why. The short version: your files stay yours.

V SHARE does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to any server. There is no cloud, no account, and no tracking. All transfers happen directly between your devices on your local network.

2. What Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or location.
  • We do not upload, copy, or store any of your files on any server.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share any information with third parties.

3. How File Sharing Works

When you share files with V SHARE, the files travel directly from one of your devices to another over your local Wi-Fi network or hotspot. The files never pass through any external server or the internet. When you close the app or stop the sharing server, the connection ends completely.

Files you receive are saved in the Downloads/V SHARE folder on your device's storage. You remain fully in control of these files at all times.

4. Permissions Used

V SHARE requests only the permissions strictly required for file sharing:

Permission Why it is needed
Storage / Photos & Media To let you browse and select files you want to send, and to save incoming files to your Downloads folder.
Nearby Wi-Fi Devices To discover other devices running V SHARE on the same local network.
Location (while in use) Android requires this permission for Wi-Fi device discovery. V SHARE never reads or stores your GPS location.
Wi-Fi / Network State To check whether your device is connected to a Wi-Fi network before starting a transfer.

5. Device Information

During discovery, devices on your local network temporarily broadcast the device name you choose (for example, "Samsung A54") so nearby devices can find each other. This information exists only within your local network for the duration of the sharing session and is never sent to us or anywhere else.

6. Children's Privacy

V SHARE does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Since no data is collected at all, the app is safe for users of all ages.

7. Third-Party Services

V SHARE does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-collection services.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
