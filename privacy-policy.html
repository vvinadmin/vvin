Privacy Policy — V VIN

Last updated: 19 September 2026

1. What information we collect

  • Account details: your name, 10-digit mobile number and (optional) email address, used to create and identify your account.
  • Referral data: your referral code and the code of the member who referred you.
  • Payment / payout details: UPI ID, bank account number, IFSC code and account holder name — entered by you for withdrawals.
  • Task submissions: photographs you upload as daily task proof (stored on our CDN).
  • Device information: a randomly generated device identifier, Firebase Cloud Messaging token (for push notifications).
  • Wallet & activity: balance, earnings, referral income, withdrawal history, task submission history and package validity dates.

2. How we use it

  • To operate the V VIN task & reward service and verify your daily submissions.
  • To process package activations, referral commissions and withdrawal payouts.
  • To send service notifications (task availability, submissions, earnings, expiry warnings).
  • To prevent fraud, duplicate accounts and payment abuse.

3. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) and Bunny CDN (task photos), protected by encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

4. What we do NOT do

  • We never sell or rent your personal data to third parties.
  • We never ask for your UPI PIN, OTP or passwords on calls/chats. Never share them.

5. Account deletion

You can delete your account any time from inside the app: Profile → DELETE MY ACCOUNT & DATA, or via our web deletion page. Deleting removes your profile, package status and login. Transaction records are retained only for payment-audit purposes.

6. Children

V VIN is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

7. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact our support: Telegram @VVinOfficial_bot or phone support shown inside the app.
