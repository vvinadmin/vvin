Answers to Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about setting up, completing digital campaigns, upgrading packages, and withdrawals.

What is the difference between Direct Referrals and Levels?

Under V VIN, direct invitees are tracked separately to keep your core recruitment metrics clean. Level 1 starts with Generation 2 (the individuals invited by your direct referrals), scaling recursively down to Level 8. This guarantees deep compensation leverage without cluttering direct recruiter tables.

How does Downline Gifting work?

If you have members in your downline who want to activate, you can select "Gift Package" directly inside the application, choose their referral code or mobile using our recursive registry search, pay via BladePay, and their package will be instantly credited.

Can I upgrade my package to a higher level?

Absolutely! Active users can choose to upgrade to any higher-tier package. The system will automatically locking out lower packages, and unlock upper ones under the "Upgrade" route.

Where do I view my salary payouts and level bonuses?

All structured compensations, sponsor match fees, team levels bonuses, and daily salaries are routed to the **"BONUSES"** history filter inside your wallet dashboard. Simple task submission rewards go directly to the **"EARNINGS"** ledger.
